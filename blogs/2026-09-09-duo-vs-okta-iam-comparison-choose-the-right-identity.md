Most teams comparing Cisco Duo and Okta are deciding between two priorities: securing the login, or governing the identity lifecycle. Duo is security-first IAM. It leads with phishing-resistant multi-factor authentication (MFA), device trust, single sign-on (SSO), and Duo Directory, its own identity provider, and it is built to protect every stage of the login journey, from enrollment to the help desk call.
