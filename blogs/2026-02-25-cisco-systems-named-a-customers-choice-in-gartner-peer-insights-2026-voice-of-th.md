Verified reviews led to Cisco Duo’s recognition as a Customers’ Choice in the Gartner® Peer Insights™ 2026 Voice of the Customer for User Authentication Report for the second year in a row. Cisco Systems once again placed in the upper-righthand quadrant with 97% of customers expressing their “Willingness to Recommend” it to their peers (based on 100 reviews as of November 2025). Placement in the Gartner Peer Insights’ 2026 Voice of the Customer Report was based on scores assigned for “User Interest and Adoption” and “Overall Experience.” Cisco’s ratings reflect 100 reviews of Duo (second highest in the category) submitted by verified customers during the 18-month period ending November 30, 2025. Overall, we feel that customers’ reviews of Duo reflect highly positive experiences. Along with their 97% willingness to recommend the solution (second highest in this year’s report), Duo’s “Product Capabilities” and “Deployment Experience” each received 4.7 out of 5 ratings (based on 99 and 94 responses, respectively, as of November 2025), the former tying for the highest rating for Product Capabilities received by any solution in the User Authentication category. From strengthening foundational access controls to leveraging the power of AI, cybersecurity and Identity and Access Management (IAM) leaders are looking to consolidate their identity security infrastructure. The Gartner Peer Insights peer reviews for Duo provide valuable input from real customers that buyers can consider before making a purchase decision. Reviews reflect Duo’s “security first” approach to protecting and managing identity – the ability to deliver both strong security and a rewarding user experience with one flexible, easy-to-use solution that includes: Duo Directory Phishing-resistant MFA Passwordless authentication Unified identity intelligence Single sign-on Highlights of peer reviews from Gartner Peer Insights include: “Reliable, Simple, and Secure” "DUO is great. I love that it protects from unauthorized access and that you get a push notification.” “Blissful Simplicity” “Seamless product, was easy to set up and configure and deliver user training." “Multi-Factor Authentication Enhances Data Security and Access Control with Duo ” "My overall experience with Duo has been exceptionally great. Duo promotes data security through a multi-factor authentication feature that allows authorized users to access using two methods to login into our systems or applications. This reduces the chances of unauthorized access." “ Truly supportive team when signed up for Duo Cares ” "Duo Care team always responds to me in a timely fashion. The relationship seems truly like we are working together rather than just a customer." "Users Appreciate App’s Reliability and Security, Prefer Hybrid Format for Use" "It is very secure, very reliable, and very user-friendly mobile app. . . good for Hybrid Mobile App User friendly."
