A non-human identity is any digital identity that authenticates without direct human interaction. Service accounts, API keys, OAuth tokens, machine identities, and AI agent credentials all fall under this category. They authenticate to systems, request data, and trigger actions, and they outnumber human identities in most modern organizations by a wide margin.
