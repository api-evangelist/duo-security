Going passwordless is one of the most impactful steps an organization can take to reduce phishing risk and simplify the authentication experience. We’ve seen broad customer adoption of Duo Push for passwordless thanks to its familiar user experience, and we’re committed to continuously improving that experience. Today, we’re introducing enhancements to Duo Passwordless Push for Duo SSO apps that address limitations of the browser cookie-based approach used to establish device trust.
